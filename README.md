## 💡 Core Philosophy: Structure Beats Intelligence

Unlike traditional, post-hoc AI alignment techniques (such as RLHF) or model-level fine-tuning, the **OMEGA Framework** operates on a fundamental thesis: **Structure Beats Intelligence**. 

Instead of modifying the weights or internal parameters of an AI model, OMEGA enforces a strict **Algorithmic Governance** layer that wraps, constraints, and orchestrates the inputs, execution paths, and output boundaries of bounded intelligence systems.

### Key Pillars
*   **Governed AI Control Plane:** Continuous, out-of-model oversight that acts as an immutable policy enforcement mechanism before action execution.
*   **Axiomatic Constraints:** Mathematical and behavioral contracts that AI agents cannot bypass, ensuring absolute safety and compliance.
*   **OMEGA Chain:** A deterministic execution ledger providing cryptographic proofs of alignment and audit logs for all autonomous decisions.
